Context

Decision 0001 merged equal subjects without reading the report. Review of #4471 found different mechanisms under one title and one-day symptoms that the existing classifier does not explain. The old triage page is also older than the layout's 180-day review threshold. Its accepted status does not make it current evidence.

Replacement rule (proposed for desk adoption)

Read the body. Resolve the tenant's locale and booking-form version; check channel, actual versus expected behaviour, and reproduction evidence. Merge only when these establish the same mechanism. A subject match is a search aid, not a verdict. Different subjects do not rule out one mechanism. Unknown tenant or conflicting symptoms means investigate separately. Count reports by unique ID, never export row.

Record why each group belongs together. Keep minority symptoms visible. Treat meta.intakeTag and the current classifier as aids to review, not proof. Prefer a separate provisional investigation over hiding unexplained reports in a majority.

Consequences

Triage takes more reading, but engineering receives distinct, auditable work. Link this record from the review without rewriting the accepted decision 0001. Desk adoption is still required; no code or prose here claims that approval.
